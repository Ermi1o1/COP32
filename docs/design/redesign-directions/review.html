What changed

1
Large titles and clear hierarchyBig bold “Today” title that shrinks into a frosted bar as you scroll. One type scale (34 / 24 / 20 / 17 / 15 / 13), like iOS Dynamic Type.
2
Deference: content firstFewer borders and colours. Teal is the only tint; lime is saved for the one main action. Grey grouped background with white cards, so sections separate without lines.
3
Grouped, inset listsShortcuts and sessions sit in rounded groups with hairline separators indented past the icon, the way iOS Settings does it.
4
Depth and materialsThe tab bar and collapsed title are translucent with blur. The hero card has a soft gradient, light shadow and layered hills, an acacia and a low sun.
5
Touch and feedbackEvery target is 44pt or larger. Buttons press in slightly; stars pop with a spring when saved. Reduced-motion settings turn this off.
6
Proper dark modeReal dark mode with true-black background and raised dark cards. Colours are retuned, not just inverted.
7
Settings & profile (your edit)New profile button next to the language switch. It opens an iOS-style sheet with your profile, sign in or sign out, My agenda, notifications, language, appearance, text size, time zone, privacy, delete my data, help and about. Accounts stay optional (guest-first, D21).
8
Programme screenTap Programme in the tab bar: segmented day picker, filter chips, and a time column with tabular numbers.

All names, people and content are invented sample data. Colours are inspired by your reference image and cic-26.com, but no COP32 logo, wordmark or tagline is copied. On iPhone the real app would use the system font; this preview uses Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for all platforms. Amharic line is machine-drafted.
