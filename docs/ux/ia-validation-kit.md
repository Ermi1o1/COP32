# IA Validation Kit — Card Sorting & Tree Testing (for the BA)
Status: ready to run anytime; **results are additional input and do not block any phase.** Expected effort: ~1 week elapsed, ~2–3 BA days.
Relates to: docs/ux/information-architecture.md (D16 v2), docs/ux/interview-questionnaire.md (same ethics rules).

## 0. What you will produce
1. Card-sort results (how people group content and what they call the groups) in EN and AM.
2. Tree-test results (can people find things in our proposed structure?).
3. A one-page summary with recommended label/structure changes.
Save everything to `docs/ux/ia-validation-results/` (create the folder). **No names or phone numbers** in files — use codes (R1, R2…).

## 1. Participants
- **8–12 for card sorting** and **10–15 for tree testing** (can overlap partly; better if different people).
- Mix: at least 3 international/visitor-type people (expats, frequent travellers, diaspora, hotel/tour staff who know visitors' questions), 3 Addis residents, 2 journalists or communications staff, 2 NGO/side-event types, 1–2 volunteers/students.
- Language: run about half in Amharic, half in English.
- Recruitment: own networks, university contacts, coworking spaces, hotel/tour contacts. No payment promised unless the founder approves; a thank-you is fine.

## 2. Ethics (read out before each session)
"We are Zega Tech, an independent company researching a possible COP32 public-information app. This is not an official COP32, UNFCCC or government product. We are testing our structure, not you — there are no wrong answers. It takes 15–25 minutes, is voluntary, and you can stop anytime. We will not record your name. May we take notes?" (Recording only with explicit consent.)

## 3. Preparation (BA + PM, ~1 day)
1. **Translate the card and tree labels into Amharic** (column provided). Use a native speaker and a second person to check; prefer plain everyday words over formal/literal translations. Record any word with two possible translations — that itself is a finding.
2. Choose a tool:
   - **Paper (recommended for in-person, zero cost):** print each card on a small card/sticky note, EN on one side, AM on the other set.
   - **Online (for remote):** a free form/spreadsheet (e.g., Google Forms/Sheets) works for tree tests; for card sorts use a free whiteboard (e.g., Miro/FigJam free tiers) or a dedicated tool's free plan (e.g., Optimal Workshop, UXtweak, Maze — **check current free limits before relying on them**).
3. Pilot once with a teammate; fix confusing card text.

## 4. Card sorting (open sort first, then closed sort)
### 4.1 Open sort (people create their own groups) — 6 participants
Instruction to participant: "Here are things you might find in a COP32 app. Put them into groups that make sense to you, then give each group a name. You can make as many groups as you like. If something doesn't fit, put it aside."

### 4.4 What to record per participant
Groups created (names + cards), cards left aside, cards hesitated on, quotes about naming. For closed sort: where each card went.

### 4.5 Analysis
1. For each card, % of participants placing it in the intended section (closed sort). **≥ 70% = fine; 50–69% = consider cross-linking; < 50% = move or rename.**
2. Open sort: list the group names people invented (in EN and AM) — candidates for better labels (especially for "Visit", "Updates", "Programme" in Amharic).
3. Note cards that people consistently pair (e.g., "Road closures" with "Getting around") — candidates for cross-links.

## 5. Tree testing (does our structure work?)
Give participants the **text-only tree** below (no visual design). For each task, they click/point through the tree to where they'd look; record the path and final answer.
### 5.1 Tree (EN; AM version to be translated)
```
Home: Next session · Alerts · Now & next · Shortcuts · Highlights
Programme: Schedule (by day) · Side events · Speakers · Exhibitors & pavilions · My agenda
Map: Venue map · City map · Directions · Accessibility
Visit: Before you travel (Visa, Flights, Insurance info) · Stay (Hotels) · Getting around (Airport, Light rail, Buses, Ride apps) · Explore Addis (Attractions, Day trips) · Coffee culture · Food · Money & SIM · Health & safety · Emergency
Updates: News · Alerts history · Press centre · Live & recorded · Explainers · Daily digest
Menu: Learn (COP explained, Climate basics, Africa & Ethiopia, Green Legacy, Glossary) · Library (Documents, Reports) · Archive · Me & Settings (Language, Time zone, Notifications, Privacy & data) · Help & FAQ · About
```
### 5.2 Tasks (with correct answers)
| # | Task (say it naturally; don't use the label words) | Correct location(s) |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | You just landed at Bole airport. Where would you look to get to your hotel? | Visit → Getting around → Airport |
| T2 | Find out whether ordinary members of the public can go to COP32 events, or only people with a special pass. | Home (Can I attend? shortcut) **or** Menu → Learn → COP explained (accept both; note which) |
| T3 | Find events happening tomorrow that are open to everyone. | Programme → Schedule (filter) / Side events |
| T4 | A session you saved has moved. Where would you check? | Home → Alerts **or** Updates → Alerts history **or** Programme → My agenda |
| T5 | You're a journalist and need today's press releases. | Updates → Press centre |
| T6 | Find a step-free entrance to the venue. | Map → Accessibility |
| T7 | You want to see a traditional coffee ceremony. | Visit → Coffee culture |
| T8 | Change the app language to Amharic. | Menu → Me & Settings → Language (or header switch) |
| T9 | Learn what "loss and damage" means. | Menu → Learn → Glossary |
| T10 | After COP32, find a recording of the opening ceremony. | Updates → Live & recorded **or** Menu → Archive |
| T11 | Apply for an Ethiopian visa. | Visit → Before you travel → Visa |
| T12 | Delete your data from the app. | Menu → Me & Settings → Privacy & data |

### 5.3 Metrics per task
- **Success rate** (correct final location). Target ≥ 80% for T1, T3, T5, T6, T8, T11; ≥ 70% for others.
- **Directness** (found without backtracking).
- **First click** (which top section they chose first) — the most important signal for tab labels.
- Time (optional).

### 5.4 Analysis
Tasks below target → look at wrong first clicks → rename, move, or add a cross-link/shortcut. Compare EN vs AM results: a label that works in EN but fails in AM needs a better Amharic word, not a structure change.

## 6. Extra quick label test (5 minutes, optional)
Show only the 5 tab names (+ icons if available later) in EN and AM; ask "What would you expect to find under each?" Record answers. Especially test the Amharic words for **Visit**, **Updates**, **Programme**.
