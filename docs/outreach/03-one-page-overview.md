# COP32 Public-Information Platform — One-page overview (DRAFT leave-behind)
*Independent proposal by Zega Tech PLC [(registration in progress)]. Not an official COP32, UNFCCC or Government product. [Date/version]*

**The need.** COP32 (Addis Ababa, November 2027 — dates and venue to be confirmed by the host) is expected to draw tens of thousands of participants and a large global audience. Accredited delegates have official, gated systems. The public, residents, journalists and visitors need one trusted, bilingual, easy source for what is happening, how to take part, how to get around Addis Ababa and what the conference decided.

**The proposal.** A free-to-use platform on Android, iOS and web, in English and Amharic:
- **Programme & side events** open to the public, with personal agenda and change alerts
- **Maps & directions** (venue and city), accessibility information, works offline
- **Visit Addis**: arrival checklist, links to the official e-visa portal, transport and ride options, accommodation links, attractions, coffee culture, safety
- **Updates**: verified news, press centre, live-stream links, plain-language explainers, daily summaries
- **Learn**: COP explained, Africa & Ethiopia climate context, Green Legacy
- **Archive & outcomes** after COP32; reusable for future events
Bookings and official processes link to official portals and approved providers; no payments handled.

**How it works with official systems.** Does not replicate delegate negotiation tools. Links to official platforms and documents. Official information is labelled by source; alerts are published only from authorised sources with dual approval.

**Principles.** Public interest · Government as intended owner [DECISION] · Privacy by design (no account needed, minimal permissions, data in Ethiopia, aligned with Proclamation 1321/2024) · Accessible and local-language first · Open, portable technology and documentation for handover · Transparent about being independent until recognised.

**Status & timeline.** Research and design complete through architecture and data strategy; demonstration with sample data [date]; public-information pilot mid-2027; event release before COP32; archive after.

**What we ask.** A meeting; guidance on the right office and process; structured public event data when ready; permission to link/embed official streams and documents.
