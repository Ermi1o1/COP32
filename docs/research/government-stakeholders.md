# Who to approach: COP32 Ethiopian government structure (researched 2026-10-01)

**Caveat:** several official pages (gcs.gov.et, pulseofafrica.info, fanamc.com) were fetched but gave little structural detail; items below mix search summaries and news. Verify before outreach.

## A/B. Reported/announced structure
- **National Steering Committee (NSC)** chaired by PM Abiy Ahmed; convened 17 Apr 2026 (gcs.gov.et — Government Communication Service). Task forces presented updates.
- Reported: **four task forces** (three confirmed active) and **13 subcommittees** under the NSC, working with the secretariat (search summary of Birr Metrics/Reporter-type sources — task-force list NOT verified).
- **COP32 President-Designate:** Dr Gedion Timothewos, Minister of Foreign Affairs.
- **COP32 Presidency Secretariat Office:** CEO **Dr Negusu Aklilu** (search summary; verify spelling/role).
- **Digital, ICT and Utilities Task Force:** briefed by **Frehiwot Tamiru, CEO of Ethio Telecom** (Fana, 18 Apr 2026). Scope per her statement: telecom, power, water/sanitation, "overall conference operations" digital system, AI + cybersecurity.
- Related national digital bodies (relevance unconfirmed): Ministry of Innovation and Technology (Minister Dr Belete Molla), Ethiopian AI Institute (DG Worku Gachena), INSA (cybersecurity; DG Tigist Hamid), Government Communication Service. None confirmed as COP32 app owners.
- Private-sector track: Addis Chamber "Green Growth Centre" for COP32 private-sector role (launch 8 Sep 2026, Addis Chamber) — a possible partner/channel, not a government owner.
