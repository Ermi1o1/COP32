# Phase 0 — Reality Check: Is an official COP32 digital platform already planned/built?

**Researched:** 2026-10-01 (web search + page fetches). **Status:** Complete, awaiting user review.
**Method caveat:** Several unfccc.int pages returned empty content to the fetch tool; UNFCCC-related claims below rest on search-result summaries and app-store listings and are marked *needs verification*.

## Verdict
**No evidence of an officially announced COP32 app, portal or digital participation platform as of 2026-10-01 — but the standard UNFCCC pattern strongly implies one WILL exist.**
Treat this as **"UNFCCC provides a standard official platform"** (the middle scenario in the brief): any project here should be positioned as **complementary/unofficial**, with explicit overlap risk. It is *not* open ground for an "official" app.

## A. Confirmed facts
- Ethiopia (Addis Ababa) is the COP32 host; endorsed by the African Group (AGN) at COP30, Belém, Nov 2025. Multiple outlets (Mongabay, Guardian NG, Africa Report, Forbes Africa via Wikipedia).
- Target timing: **November 2027** (no exact dates found in any source).
- Foreign Minister Gedion Timothewos is COP32 President-Designate (ethioembassy.ca; several news sources).
- National steering/organizing committee chaired by PM Abiy Ahmed (Dawan Africa, 2026-04-18; amn.gov.et).
- COP30 had an official "COP30 Event Platform" mobile app (Android `com.unfccc.cop30`, iOS id6752987607), EN + PT, agenda, live/on-demand sessions, networking (store listings via search).
- COP28 had two official apps: "UN Climate Change App" (UNFCCC) and "COP28 UAE App" (host government, venue navigation) — unfccc.int/cop28/mobile (search summary).

## B. Officially announced
- A **Digital, ICT and Utilities Task Force** exists for COP32; Ethio Telecom CEO Frehiwot Tamiru (Fana, 2026-04-18) said a "comprehensive digital system" will manage telecom, power, water/sanitation and **overall conference operations**, with AI and cybersecurity. This is infrastructure/operations — **not** a participant-facing app announcement.
- cop32ethiopia.org: "Site is undergoing maintenance… available soon", with a login/password-recovery UI present (suggests a registration/account system is planned; unconfirmed).

## C. Reported but unconfirmed
- Attendance ~50,000 (press only).
- cic-26.com presents as a COP32 info site (Addis address, info@cop32ethiopia.org) but does not name its operator; **official status unverified**. Possible unofficial/third-party — do not rely on it.
- Search summary says COP30 platform was built by the UNFCCC Digital Participation Team with **Social27** — not verified on primary page.

## D. Analysis
- UNFCCC consistently ships its own participant event platform + app (COP28 → COP30), gated by badge type. Host governments sometimes add a venue/logistics app (COP28 UAE). Expect the same split for COP32: UNFCCC platform (registered delegates) + Ethiopian host app/portal (logistics, visitors).
- The gap an independent product could fill is therefore **public/non-accredited audiences, Ethiopia-local context, Amharic, low-bandwidth/offline, post-event archive** — not the delegate agenda/networking core, which the official platform will own and gate.
- Biggest dependency: official data (schedule, venues, accreditation) is controlled by UNFCCC/host; no public API found.

## E. Assumptions
- A host-government digital layer will emerge (uncertain timing/scope).
- Official UNFCCC platform usually launches months before the COP (COP32 would be ~mid-2027).

## F. Still needs verification
- Primary UNFCCC pages for COP28–30 apps and vendor (fetch failed).
- COP29 (Baku) app details (not retrieved).
- Whether cop32ethiopia.org / cic-26.com are official and who operates them.
- Procurement: searches of tender aggregators found **no COP32 digital/app tender**; eGP (egp.gov.et) and PPPA not searched directly (aggregators only) — absence not proven.
- Exact COP32 dates/venue; COP32 host-country agreement with UNFCCC.
- Whether any Ethiopian ministry (MinT, Ethio Telecom, ICT) has a COP32 app mandate.
- Social media from official accounts (not searched).

## Options for you
1. **Proceed as complementary/unofficial platform** (recommended): public-facing, Ethiopia-local, Amharic, offline-first, post-event archive. Re-check quarterly.
2. **Pursue partnership first**: approach the host Digital/ICT Task Force / MinT before investing; may turn this into an official extension. Higher upside, slower, dependent on access.
3. **Pause/pivot** if you intended an *official* delegate-facing app — that niche is almost certainly taken.
