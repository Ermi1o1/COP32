**D:** The ">80,000" you cited matches the Mayor's figure — keep it as the *upper planning case*. Real attendance is usually below registration; COP30 in-person was ~76% of registered, and party-badge holders only ~13% of attendance -> **most attendees are observers, media, staff, side-event/public visitors** — supports the target audience (D2/primary users).

## 4. Logistics
- Accommodation: ~25,000 beds now; target >80,000 via new hotels, guesthouses, serviced apartments (C). Pipeline: 34 hotels / 5,964 rooms under development, ~80% under construction (ENA/Addis Insight, Mar 2026). MIDROC ~1,500 beds; 150-room Hilton Garden Inn due Oct 2027 (C).
- Visa: Ethiopia e-visa exists (evisa.gov.et); UNFCCC delegations are usually advised to e-visa + invitation letter (older UNECA/UNFCCC info notes). **F:** COP32-specific visa/host-country arrangements.
- Registration/accreditation: UNFCCC runs badge accreditation (Parties, observers, media) — **F** for COP32 portal and dates.
- D: COP30 showed accommodation shortfalls depress attendance; Addis has the same risk -> accommodation/transport information is a strong public use case.

## 5. Known structure of a COP (general UNFCCC knowledge — verify against COP32 pages)
Two-week session; Blue Zone (UN-managed negotiations, badge-gated) vs Green Zone (public/host-managed events at some COPs); Subsidiary Bodies sessions in Bonn mid-year (SB62 ~June 2026; SB64 ~June 2027 — **F: dates**); Pre-COP; side events/pavilions; Action Agenda; mandatory online participation options as at COP30.
